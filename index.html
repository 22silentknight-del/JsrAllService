01 — ABOUT THE COMPANY

A professionally managed geospatial partner, built on field-verified accuracy.

JSR Technology and Services is a professionally managed surveying and geospatial solutions company delivering high-accuracy data and dependable outputs across infrastructure, land development, and regulatory projects. Our core capabilities include LiDAR surveys, DGPS, Total Station surveys, physical site measurements, topographic mapping, and 2D/3D CAD drawings, supported by advanced workflows and stringent quality controls.

We also specialize in 3D virtual tours, Matterport-based documentation, and digital site visualization, enabling clients to gain precise spatial understanding for planning, monitoring, and presentation purposes. With deep expertise in airport surveys and NOC-related documentation, we ensure full compliance with statutory and aviation authority requirements.
